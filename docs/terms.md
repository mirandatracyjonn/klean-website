3.2 **Payment before use.** A machine is activated only after your payment is confirmed by the payment provider
(or, when you pay cash at a laundromat's counter, after the attendant records your cash payment in Klean), and it starts only when you start it (in the app or with the machine's Start button); make sure your laundry is
inside and the door is closed first.

activated and the other loads are kept as load credits (3.3). Where a laundromat has a **Klean Scale**, the weight it
reports is used for your loads; check the number before you pay.

3.2a **Receipts.** Every paid order has a Klean receipt in the app (Account → Receipts) and on klean.ph, which you can

3.3 **Load credits.** Extra loads you paid for, saved dries, and loads whose machine couldn't be activated are kept as

3.4 **Points.** Operators may offer loyalty points ("**Klean Points**"). Points are earned and redeemable **only

change it for the future; points already earned keep their value until they expire. If an Operator closes its
business on Klean, its points and free loads end; paid loads must be used or refunded before it can close (4.8).

3.5 **Problems with a wash.** Report machine problems to the Operator's staff. Klean returns credits
automatically when a machine fails to start after payment. Questions about laundry quality, damage or lost items

## 7. Fees, refunds and chargebacks

Our [Return & Refund Policy](refund-policy.md) explains these rules in short.

7.1 Laundry payments are made to the Operator through Klean. Refund requests for laundry services go to the
Operator (message them in the app, or ask an attendant), who decides in line with law and their policies. Klean may
issue refunds where the Service itself failed (e.g. a double charge).

7.2 **How refunds work.** An Operator can refund some or all loads of a paid order within 90 days of payment. The
money goes back to the payment method you used, through our payment partners (GCash and Maya usually within a few
days, cards up to about two weeks, depending on your bank). The refunded loads' unused washes and dries are cancelled,
and the points that payment earned are taken back in proportion. If you paid **cash** at the counter, the refund is
handed back in cash by the laundromat. Refunds of Beta (test) payments move only test money.

7.3 **Operators and refunds.** A refund (less Klean's commission on it, which Klean returns) is deducted from the
Operator's next payout. A cash refund is handed back by the Operator from its own cash; Klean returns its commission on
it in the next payout. The payment processor's fee on the original payment isn't returned by the processor and isn't
refunded to the Operator. If a payment is charged back, related points and credits may be reversed in the same way.

7.4 **Chargebacks.** If a customer disputes a payment with their bank or card issuer, Klean tells the Operator, who
should send Klean any evidence (photos, messages, the machine's start and finish times) within 5 days; Klean answers
the bank as the payment account holder. If the bank decides for the customer, the disputed order is treated as a full
refund of the loads not yet refunded: its unused washes and dries are cancelled, its points are taken back, Klean
returns its commission on it, and the amount is deducted from the Operator's next payout. If the bank decides for the
Operator, nothing changes. A customer who wins a chargeback isn't refunded a second time.
