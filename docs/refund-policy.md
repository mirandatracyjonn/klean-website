# Klean by Geerho Return & Refund Policy

*Effective date: October 22, 2026. Version 1.0.*

This policy explains how refunds work for payments made through **Klean by Geerho** ("Klean"), owned and operated by
**Geerho Tech LLC**. It summarises the refund rules in our [Terms of Service](terms.md) (sections 3 and 7); if the two
ever differ, the Terms apply. Nothing here limits the rights you have under the law where you live, including the
Consumer Act of the Philippines.

## 1. Returns

Klean doesn't sell physical goods, so there is nothing to ship back. Laundry, carwash and motorcycle wash services are
provided by independent businesses ("**Operators**") that use Klean to take payments. Refunds work as described below.

## 2. Asking for a refund

- **For a laundry, carwash or motorcycle wash service:** ask the Operator. Message them in the app (their page →
  **Message**), or ask an attendant at the counter. The Operator decides in line with the law and its own policies.
- **When Klean itself failed** (for example, you were charged twice for the same order): email **info@klean.ph**
  with your receipt (Account → Receipts). Klean refunds payments the Service got wrong.

## 3. Time limits and where the money goes

- An Operator can refund some or all loads of a paid order **within 90 days** of payment.
- Money goes back to **the payment method you used**: GCash and Maya usually within a few days; cards up to about two
  weeks, depending on your bank.
- If you paid **cash** at the counter, the laundromat hands the refund back in cash.
- Refunds of Beta (test) payments move only test money.

## 4. What happens to credits and points

- The refunded loads' unused washes and dries are cancelled.
- The Klean Points that payment earned are taken back in proportion.

## 5. When a machine doesn't start

If a machine can't be activated after you've paid, Klean automatically returns the load to your wallet as a
**load credit**, usable on any available machine of the same Operator. Load credits expire **30 days** after purchase
and have no cash value. Report machine problems to the Operator's staff.

## 6. Wash bays

- **Self-service bays:** you pay for time. Unused minutes aren't refunded once the bay has started, and time can't be
  moved to another bay. If a bay doesn't start after you've paid, ask the Operator.
- **Full-service washes:** ask the Operator about any problem with the wash; refunds follow section 3.

## 7. Disputes with your bank (chargebacks)

If you dispute a payment with your bank or card issuer, the Operator may send evidence and Klean answers the bank. If
the bank decides for you, the order is treated as a full refund of the loads not yet refunded (section 4). A customer
who wins a chargeback isn't refunded a second time.

## 8. App Store and Google Play purchases

Klean currently has no paid in-app purchases (identity verification is free). If that changes, purchases made through
Apple or Google are refunded under their rules: ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com or Google Play at
play.google.com/store/account.

## 9. Operators

A refund (less Klean's commission on it, which Klean returns) is deducted from the Operator's next payout. See the
Terms of Service, section 7.3.
