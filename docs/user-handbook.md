# Klean User Manual

Welcome to Klean. This guide covers everything you need as a customer: signing up, washing, paying, and earning
free loads. Klean works on iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, and in your web browser.

## Getting started

### 1. Sign in

Open Klean and choose **Continue with Apple**, **Continue with Google**, or **Continue with GitHub**.

Klean is **one person, one account**. If you've used Klean before, sign in the same way you did then. You can
add other sign-in methods later in **Account → Sign-in methods**.

### 2. Verify your identity

Everyone on Klean verifies once: customers, staff and owners alike. It keeps accounts real and stops points abuse.

1. Pay the verification fee: a small in-app purchase in the iPhone or Android app (**US$2.99**, or **₱199** in the
   Philippines, shown in your local price), or on klean.ph **US$2.99** by card or **₱199** with GCash, Maya or QR Ph.
2. Tap **Start verification** and follow the steps: hold a valid ID in front of the camera, then look at the camera
   for a quick selfie. There's no button to press: each photo is taken automatically once it's steady.
   For a quick check, use good light without glare, lay your ID flat on a dark surface, and remove glasses, hats and
   masks for the selfie.
3. Most people are approved within a minute or two. Some checks take up to a day; we'll let you know.

Good to know:

- The fee is charged **per attempt** and is **non-refundable**. If you close the verification window before
  finishing, tap **Continue verification** to reopen it at no extra cost.
- Use a valid, unexpired ID, in good light, with nothing covering your face.
- We don't keep a copy of your ID. Our verification partner, Didit, handles the images.
- If we find you already have a Klean account, tap **Use my existing account** to continue with it.

## Washing and drying

### Self-service: scan a machine

1. Go to a machine that isn't running.
2. Tap **Scan**, and point your camera at the QR code on the machine.
3. Pay for one load: one wash and one dry. The machine is held for you for about 10 minutes while you pay.
4. When payment clears, the machine is ready. **Press Start on the machine.**
5. Your dry is saved in your wallet. When the wash is done, scan any free dryer at the same laundromat business.
   No second payment.

If the camera doesn't work, type the code printed under the QR.

### With an attendant

1. Hand your laundry to the attendant. They'll weigh it and tell you how many loads you need (one load per 8 kg,
   or your laundromat's limit).
2. Scan the payment QR on their screen with your phone and pay.
3. The attendant assigns your machines. Press **Start** when the light turns white.

To earn points on attendant-assisted visits, give the attendant the **member code** from **Account**. They'll
add it to your order.

### After you pay

The app tells you when your machine is ready. Press **Start** on the machine within a few minutes. If the
machine shows an error, ask an attendant: your credit is safe.

### If something goes wrong

- **You paid but the machine didn't start:** check Home. If you see a wash credit, your money is safe. Scan any
  free washer at the same business and it starts without another payment.
- **The machine shows red after you paid:** the credit goes back to your wallet automatically. Use another
  machine, or ask an attendant.
- Load credits are valid for **30 days**.

## Points and free loads

- You earn points every time you pay: usually **10 points per load**, plus a **10-point welcome bonus** on your
  first paid visit to a laundromat business.
- **100 points = a free wash + dry** (each business can set its own numbers; Home shows them).
- **Points belong to the business where you earned them.** Points at one business work at all its branches,
  but not at a different business.
- Claim a free load from **Home → Claim free load**. It's added to your wallet; scan a free washer to use it.
- Points expire **12 months** after you earn them. Free loads don't earn points.
- **Welcome load:** new to Klean? Some laundromats give brand-new members their first wash + dry free. If you
  verified in the last 30 days and haven't done laundry on Klean yet, just scan a machine at a participating
  laundromat: it starts without payment. One welcome load per person. Verification is once for all of Klean; you
  never verify again for another laundromat.

## Your account

- **Member code** (Account): a code like `KLN-7H3K-Q9MP`. Give it to an attendant to earn points, or to a
  laundromat that wants to hire you. It can't be used to sign in or pay.
- **Sign-in methods:** link Apple, Google and GitHub to the same account.
- **Start a laundromat business:** see the Super Admin Manual.
- **Delete account:** Account → Delete account. This removes your personal data and forfeits unused points and
  credits. It can't be undone.

## Internet connection

Klean needs the internet to pay and start machines. If you lose connection, the app shows **No internet
connection** until you're back on Wi-Fi or mobile data. A machine that's already running isn't affected.
