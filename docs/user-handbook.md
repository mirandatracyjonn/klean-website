# Klean by Geerho User Manual

Welcome to Klean by Geerho. This guide covers everything you need as a customer: signing up, washing, paying, and earning
free loads. Klean works on iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, and in your web browser.

## Getting started

### 1. Sign in

Open Klean and choose **Continue with Apple**, **Continue with Google**, or **Continue with GitHub**.

Klean is **one person, one account**. If you've used Klean before, sign in the same way you did then. You can
add other sign-in methods later in **Account → Sign-in methods**.

### 2. Verify your identity

Everyone on Klean verifies once: customers, staff and owners alike. It keeps accounts real and stops points abuse.

1. Verification is **free**: Klean pays for the identity check. In a web browser you can also verify at
   **klean.ph/account.html**.
2. Tap **Start verification** and follow the steps: hold a valid ID in front of the camera, then look at the camera
   for a quick selfie. There's no button to press: each photo is taken automatically once it's steady.
   For a quick check, use good light without glare, lay your ID flat on a dark surface, and remove glasses, hats and
   masks for the selfie.
3. Most people are approved within a minute or two. After the last step the app shows **Checking your
   verification…**: keep Klean open and don't start again. Some checks take up to a day; we'll let you know.

Good to know:

- If you close the verification window before finishing, tap **Continue verification** to reopen it.
- Use a valid, unexpired ID, in good light, with nothing covering your face.
- We don't keep a copy of your ID. Our verification partner, Didit, handles the images.
- If we find you already have a Klean account, tap **Use my existing account** to continue with it.
- Once you're verified, you stay verified.

## Washing and drying

### Find a laundromat near you

Tap **Laundromats near you** on Home (or **Find a laundromat** on klean.ph). Allow location and Klean shows the nearest
laundromats on a map and in a list, with how many washers and dryers are free right now, the price, and **Directions**.
Your location is only used to sort the list; Klean doesn't store it.

### Self-service: start laundry (weigh, pay, scan)

1. At the laundromat, tap **Start laundry** on Home and choose the laundromat you're at. Choose what you need:
   **Wash + dry**, **Wash only** or **Dry only** (each laundromat sets its prices; Start laundry shows them).
2. Weigh your dirty laundry on the laundromat's scale and enter the kilos (or choose the number of loads). Klean works
   out the loads: for example 9 kg with an 8 kg limit is **2 loads**.
3. Pay once. The scanner opens: scan a free washer, load your laundry and close the door, then tap **Start wash**
   on Home (or press Start on the machine). Machines never start by themselves. Klean goes back to Home, which shows
   what's still paid; tap **Scan a washer** for the next load.
4. When a wash is done you get a notification: move the clothes to a free dryer, tap **Scan a dryer**, scan it, then tap
   **Start dry** (or press Start on the dryer). You're notified again when the dry is done.

### Self-service: scan a machine first

1. Go to a machine that isn't running.
2. Tap **Scan**, and point your camera at the QR code on the machine.
3. Scanned a washer? Choose **Wash + dry** or **Wash only**. Scanned a dryer with nothing saved? It's **Dry only**.
   Scanned a dryer but you only have washes paid? Klean asks if you're sure; **Back** if you meant a washer.
   Choose how many loads (up to 5) and pay once. The machine is held for you for
   about 10 minutes while you pay.
4. When payment clears, the machine is ready (its light turns white). Load your laundry, close the door, then tap
   **Start wash** / **Start dry** on Home and confirm, or **press Start on the machine**. A machine never starts by
   itself.
5. Chose wash + dry? Your dry is saved in your wallet. When the wash is done, scan any free dryer at the same laundromat business.
   No second payment. Home shows what's paid ("Your dry is paid", or "2 washes and 3 dries paid").
6. Paid for more than one load? Scan the next free washer: it starts without payment. Saved loads last 30 days.

If the camera doesn't work, type the code printed under the QR. Some machines also have an **NFC sticker** beside the QR:
just touch it with your unlocked phone and Klean opens on that machine (iPhone: or tap **Tap NFC sticker** on Scan).

### With an attendant

1. Hand your laundry to the attendant. They'll weigh it and tell you how many loads you need (one load per 8 kg,
   or your laundromat's limit).
2. Scan the payment QR on their screen with your phone and pay.
3. The attendant assigns your machines. Press **Start** when the light turns white.

To earn points on attendant-assisted visits, give the attendant the **member code** from **Account**. They'll
add it to your order.

### Your Klean card in Apple Wallet

Account → **Add to Apple Wallet**. Show its QR at the counter so the attendant can add points to your visit. It shows
your points when you added it; add it again to refresh. (Google Wallet for Android is coming.)

### Klean on your Home Screen

Add the **Klean widget**: iPhone: touch and hold the Home Screen → **Edit** → **Add Widget** → Klean (small or medium; also
on the Lock Screen). Android: touch and hold the Home Screen → **Widgets** → Klean. It shows your laundromat's free
washers and dryers (live), your points, what's paid, and when a running machine will be done. Tap it to open Klean.

### Time left on your wash or dry

Once your machine starts, Klean shows a countdown: on iPhone on the **Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island**
("W1 · Washing · 32:10"), on Android as a notification that stays until the cycle ends. It updates by itself; when the
cycle is done you also get a notification. (iPhone: Settings → Klean → Live Activities must be on.)

### After you pay

The app tells you when your machine is ready. Press **Start** on the machine within a few minutes. If the
machine shows an error, ask an attendant: your credit is safe.

### If something goes wrong

- **You paid but the machine didn't start:** check Home. If you see a wash credit, your money is safe. Scan any
  free washer at the same business and it starts without another payment.
- **The machine shows red after you paid:** the credit goes back to your wallet automatically. Use another
  machine, or ask an attendant.
- Load credits are valid for **30 days**.

### Faster payments

Your name comes from your verified ID, so the payment page fills it in for you. Save your mobile number (your GCash or
Maya number) in **Account → Mobile number** and it's filled in too. You can change or remove it any time. GCash and Maya
may still ask you to log in to approve the payment; that's their own security.

## Points and free loads

- You earn points every time you pay: usually **10 points per load**, plus a **10-point welcome bonus** on your
  first paid visit to a laundromat business.
- **100 points = a free wash + dry** (each business can set its own numbers; Home shows them).
- **Points belong to the business where you earned them.** Points at one business work at all its branches,
  but not at a different business.
- Claim a free load from **Home → Claim free load**. It's added to your wallet; scan a free washer to use it.
- Points expire **12 months** after you earn them. Free loads don't earn points.
- **Welcome load:** new to Klean? Some laundromats give brand-new members their first wash + dry free. If you
  verified in the last 30 days and haven't done laundry on Klean yet, just scan a machine at a participating
  laundromat: it starts without payment. One welcome load per person. Verification is once for all of Klean; you
  never verify again for another laundromat.

## Your account

- **Member code** (Account): a code like `KLN-7H3K-Q9MP`. Give it to an attendant to earn points, or to a
  laundromat that wants to hire you. It can't be used to sign in or pay.
- **Sign-in methods:** link Apple, Google and GitHub to the same account.
- **Start a laundromat business:** see the Super Admin Manual.
- **Delete account:** Account → Delete account. This removes your personal data and forfeits unused points and
  credits. It can't be undone.

## Internet connection

Klean needs the internet to pay and start machines. If you lose connection, the app shows **No internet
connection** until you're back on Wi-Fi or mobile data. A machine that's already running isn't affected.
