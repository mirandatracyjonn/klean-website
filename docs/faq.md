# Klean by Geerho FAQ

## For customers

**Why do I have to verify my ID?**
Klean is one person, one account, for everyone: customers, staff and owners. Verification keeps points fair,
stops fraud, and protects your wallet.

**Does verification cost anything?**
No. Verification is free: Klean pays for the identity check. If you close the window halfway, you can reopen it.

**Do I have to verify again later?**
No. Once you're verified, you stay verified, even if Klean ever adds a fee for new verifications.

**Do you keep a copy of my ID?**
No. Our verification partner, Didit, handles the images. Klean keeps only your verification result, your verified
name and country, and one-way codes used to detect duplicate accounts.

**It says I already have an account.**
Tap **Use my existing account** to move this sign-in method onto your original account. If that doesn't work,
contact support.

**I paid but the machine didn't start.**
Your money is safe: a wash credit is waiting on **Home**. Scan any free washer at the same laundromat business and it
starts without another payment.

**Do I have to type my name every time I pay?**
No. Your verified name and email are filled in on the payment page. Save your mobile number in **Account → Mobile
number** and that's filled in too. GCash or Maya may still ask you to log in to approve.

**Do I need the app to pay for a machine?**
No. Scan the machine's QR code with your phone's camera and tap **Pay in your browser** on klean.ph. Sign in, verify
once if you're new, pay, then tap **Start wash** (or **Start dry**) on the same page. The app is faster next time.

**Can I get a refund?**
Ask the laundromat: message them in the app (Home → the laundromat → Message) or ask an attendant. Only the owner can
refund. A refund goes back to how you paid (GCash/Maya usually within days, cards up to 2 weeks); you get a
notification, the refunded loads' unused washes/dries are cancelled, and the points they earned are taken back.

**It says "Test payment: no real money". What does that mean?**
That laundromat is still testing Klean (Beta). Nothing is charged: on the payment page, use PayMongo's test options
(for example the test GCash button) or, where checkout is by Stripe, the test card 4242 4242 4242 4242 with any future
date and any CVC. Points and loads from test payments are for testing only.

**How do I find a laundromat near me?**
On Home, tap **Laundromats near you** (or **Find a laundromat** on klean.ph). Allow location to see the nearest ones on
a map with free washers and dryers right now, and tap **Directions**.

**Can I see how long my wash has left?**
Yes. Once it starts, the time left shows on your iPhone's Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, or in a notification on
Android, and you're notified when it's done. iPhone: Settings → Klean → Live Activities must be on.

**Is there a Klean widget?**
Yes: add it from your Home Screen's widget list (iPhone: also on the Lock Screen). It shows free machines at your
laundromat, your points, what's paid, and when your machine will be done.

**Can I pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay?**
At Philippine laundromats you pay on PayMongo's page with GCash, Maya, QR Ph or card. Google Pay and Apple Pay
aren't available there yet; we'll add them when our payment provider turns them on. At laundromats that use card payments through
Stripe (for example in the US), Apple Pay and Google Pay appear on the payment page automatically.

**Can I message the laundromat?**
Yes: open the laundromat's page (from Home or Laundromats near you) and tap **Message**. Its staff reply in Klean and you
get a notification. Your conversations are under Home → **Messages**. Laundromats you've used can also post
announcements ("Closed Monday"), which appear on Home. When your wash or dry is done, the update arrives in your chat
with that laundromat; tap the notification and answer with one tap (**I'm on my way**, **I'll pick it up later**,
**Thank you!**). To report a message, touch and hold it (iPhone) or tap it
(Android) → **Report**.

**Does Klean work with Siri?**
Yes: "How long is left on my laundry in Klean?", "How many washers are free at Klean?", "Show my Klean member code",
"Scan a machine in Klean". On Android, touch and hold the Klean icon for quick actions.

**Can I put my Klean card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet?**
Yes: Account → **Add to Apple Wallet** (iPhone) or **Save to Google Wallet** (Android), also on klean.ph → Account.
The points on the card update by themselves when you earn or use points.
It shows your member code as a QR for the counter, your points and your laundromat. Add it again to refresh the
points.

**What's the round sticker next to the QR?**
An NFC sticker. Touch it with your unlocked phone and Klean opens on that machine (iPhone: or tap **Tap NFC sticker**
on Scan). If a sticker doesn't open Klean, scan the QR instead and tell the staff.

**Will the machine start by itself after I pay?**
No. For safety, a machine only starts when you start it: tap **Start wash** or **Start dry** on Home (Klean asks
you to check that your laundry is inside and the door is closed), or press Start on the machine.

**Can I just wash, or just dry?**
Yes, where the laundromat offers it. Choose **Wash only** or **Dry only** in Start laundry, or when you scan a washer.
Scanning a dryer with nothing saved gives you a dry-only load. Prices are on the laundromat's page. A wash-only load works only on washers and a dry-only load only on dryers, because their prices
differ: scanning a dryer when you only have washes paid first asks "You already paid for a washer. Are you sure you
want to use this dryer?" Tap **Back** if you meant a washer; **Yes, use dryer** asks you to pay for a dry, and your
paid washes stay saved.

**How do I dry my clothes?**
Every wash + dry load includes a dry. When your wash finishes, scan any free dryer at the same business. Home shows
"Your dry is paid" with a **Scan a dryer** button.

**I have lots of laundry. Do I pay for each washer?**
No. After you scan the first washer, choose how many loads (up to 5) and pay once. That washer starts; your other
washes and all your dries are saved in your wallet. Scan each free washer or dryer and it starts with no payment.

**How long do credits last?**
30 days from purchase.

**How do points work?**
Usually 10 points per paid load, plus a 10-point welcome bonus on your first paid visit to each business. 100
points = a free wash + dry. Each business can set its own numbers; Home shows them.

**Do new members get a free wash?**
At laundromats that offer a welcome load, yes: if you verified in the last 30 days and haven't done laundry on Klean
yet, your first wash + dry there is free. One per person across Klean.

**Do I verify again at a different laundromat?**
No. You verify once, and your account works at every Klean laundromat.

**Can I use points at a different laundromat?**
Only at branches of the same business where you earned them. Points from different businesses are kept separate.

**Do points expire?**
Yes, 12 months after you earn them.

**Can I get a receipt?**
Yes. Every paid visit has one in Account → **Receipts** (and on klean.ph → Account). Share it, print it or save it as
a PDF. It isn't a BIR official receipt; ask the laundromat if you need one.

**Can I pay cash?**
At the counter, yes: hand the cash to the attendant, who records it in Klean and gives you your change before
assigning your machines. Self-service (scanning a machine yourself) takes GCash, Maya, QR Ph and cards in the
Philippines, and cards elsewhere.

**Why won't the app open without internet?**
Klean needs a live connection to take payments and start machines. Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data and it unlocks
automatically. Machines already running aren't affected.

**How do I delete my account?**
Account → Delete account. Unused points and credits are forfeited.

**Why does Klean ask me to update?**
When a new version fixes something important, Klean shows "Update available", or "Update required" for older
versions that can't be used any more. Tap Update (App Store, Google Play or TestFlight). Turning on automatic updates
on your phone keeps you current without asking.

## For laundromat owners (Super Admins)

**How much does Klean cost?**
Free to download and set up, with no subscription. Klean takes a commission on each payment it processes (shown
in Business → Setup). Payment-processor fees apply. Free loads redeemed with points carry no commission.

**What hardware do I need?**
One Klean controller box per 4 coin-operated machines, plus a current sensor per machine: roughly ₱1,500–3,000 per
machine in parts, bought ready-made or built yourself. Klean HQ's Hardware page and the Hardware Guide list the
parts and show how to wire them. Your machines keep accepting coins.

**Can I try Klean before installing anything?**
Yes. Every business starts in **Beta**: payments use test money, nothing is charged or paid out, and you can delete
the test data any time. Open Klean HQ on a computer, add a location with simulated washers and dryers, and scan
their QR codes with the Klean app.

**How do I start taking real payments?**
Business → Setup → switch **Beta testing** off. You need your payout account set up and at least one real machine
connected with a Klean controller. Going live deletes your beta data.

**Can I change the commission?**
No. Only Klean sets the commission rate, and you'll get at least 30 days' notice of any increase. Each transaction
uses the rate in effect when it was made.

**When do I get paid?**
Klean pays you automatically once a day (around 2:00 a.m. Manila time): the day's sales minus Klean's commission,
the payment processor's fees, and a ₱10 transfer fee in the Philippines. In the Philippines it goes to your GCash,
Maya or bank account (in your verified name); elsewhere, to your Stripe account.

**How do I add staff?**
They install Klean and verify their identity, then give you their member code. Enter it in Business → Staff.

**Can a staff member work at more than one branch?**
Yes: assign them to any number of branches, or to all of them. Change it anytime.

**Can I see who my customers are?**
You see display names, masked emails, points, visits, spend and usage times for people who use your laundromats.
You don't see their ID or legal name.

**Which reports can I get?**
Daily, weekly, semi-monthly, monthly, quarterly, semi-annual and annual sales, with CSV export; cash taken per
attendant and branch; and expenses with profit (Business → Expenses).

**How do I pay Klean's commission on cash sales?**
It comes off your next payout of GCash, Maya and card sales. If you take mostly cash, pay it in Business → Sales →
**Commission on cash sales** → **Pay** (GCash, Maya or card). If any of it is 30 days unpaid, recording cash pauses
until it's paid; we remind you 7 days before.

**How do attendants close a shift?**
Work → Intake → **Count my cash and close my shift**: they count the cash they took and enter it, without seeing
what Klean expects. Klean shows the difference, and Super Admins are told about any shortfall (Business → Sales →
Shift counts).

**Can Klean remind us about detergent and machine cleaning?**
Yes. Work → **Supplies**: list each branch's supplies with a "low at" level (and, if you like, how much a wash or dry
uses); staff record restocks, and everyone is reminded when one runs low. Work → **Maintenance**: routine tasks (lint
filter every 10 dryer cycles, drum and door seal weekly, hoses and vents monthly, or your own); staff tap Done, log
repairs, and get a morning reminder when something's due. Each machine keeps its history.

**Can my attendants take cash?**
Yes, at the counter: they record it in Work (**Paying cash instead** → **Cash received**), and only then can machines be
assigned, so cash that isn't rung up can't run a machine. Business → Sales → **Cash by person** shows who took how
much. Cash sales carry the same commission, deducted from your next payout.

**Can I track my expenses?**
Yes: Business → **Expenses**. Log utilities, detergent, rent, repairs, wages and more; Klean shows each period's
expenses by category and your profit (net sales minus expenses).

**What happens if the shop internet goes down?**
Klean locks on every device until it's back online, and machine controllers show red. Keep reliable Wi-Fi, ideally
with a mobile-data backup.

## For attendants

**Do I need a separate staff account?**
No. Your one personal Klean account gets staff access when your employer adds you. If you leave the job, your
customer account stays yours.

**A machine is red. What do I do?**
Read the reason on the card, check the machine, then tap Clear error. See the Moderator Handbook.

**Can I ring up my own laundry?**
No. Ask another attendant, or use self-service scan like any customer.

**What happens if my laundromat closes on Klean?**
Its points and free loads end, and you can't scan its machines any more. A laundromat can only close after every
customer's paid loads there are used or refunded, so you never lose a load you paid for.

**How does Klean know how many loads I need?**
Weigh your laundry on the laundromat's scale and enter the kilos in **Start laundry**. Each load is up to the
laundromat's limit (usually 8 kg), so 9 kg is 2 loads. Pay once, then scan a washer for each load and a dryer for each dry.

**What is the Klean Scale?**
Some laundromats have a Klean Scale: it sends the weight straight to Klean. Put your laundry on it, wait for the number
to settle, then in **Start laundry** tap **Use the Klean Scale** and the kilos fill in by themselves. Each weighing can
be used once, within 2 minutes. Some laundromats require it, so typing a weight isn't possible there.
