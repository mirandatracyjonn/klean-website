How it works

Laundry in three taps

Every Klean washer and dryer has its own QR code. Your phone does the rest.

STEP 1

Scan

Open Klean and scan the sticker on any free machine. Its light shows green when it's yours to use.

STEP 2

Pay

GCash, Maya, QR Ph, card or Google Pay. One price covers the wash and the dry. Lots of laundry? Choose up to 5 loads and pay once.

STEP 3

Done

Load up, close the door, then tap Start in Klean or press Start on the machine. It never starts by itself. Klean tells you when the wash is done, and when your laundry is dry and ready.
